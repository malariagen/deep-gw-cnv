Experiment 40 results summary:
- HMM transitions p50 dropped only 18%: 1189 → 970. Expected O(10–100); actual still too high.
- Sanity check (≥55% CN=1) continues to mask 40–67% of GT CNV positives; total_fnr=0.41–0.71.
- Evaluable-subset FNR remains excellent: GCH1=0.09, MDR1=0.02, PM2_PM3=0.04, CRT=0.11.

My diagnosis:
The 0.90 → 0.96 change moved (1-p) from 0.10 to 0.04 — a 4× reduction in transition
probability — yet transitions dropped only 18%. The 06_conv_vae residuals are fundamentally
noisier than the VAE used in exp 30/31 where 0.96 produced p50=270. With the new model,
the HMM emission variance is large enough that the state-transition prior barely constrains
oscillation at 0.96. The sanity check keeps firing on the same majority of samples because
p50=970 is still far above the threshold at which ≥55% CN=1 coverage is achievable. All
blocked samples still have strong CRR signal (p25=1.75–2.00), confirming the issue is
purely in the HMM calibration, not in the coverage data.

Proposed experiment 41: raise hmm_self_transition 0.96 → 0.99
Changes from exp 40:
- hmm_self_transition: 0.96 → 0.99 (1-p drops 4×: 0.04 → 0.01)
- HMM segmentation re-run (segments.parquet not symlinked); VAE artifacts reused from exp 37

What I expect:
At (1-p)=0.01, empirical extrapolation from the 0.90→0.96 ratio puts transitions p50 at
~240 — close to exp 30's 270, where sanity checks passed and masked_cnv_rate was low. Once
sanity passes, the stickier HMM will leave segment_cn2_fraction=0 for many FNs, but CRR
rescue at 1.55 catches them directly (masked CRR p25=1.75–2.00 across all genes). Total_fnr
should then converge toward the evaluable FNR (0.02–0.11). PPV may shift as the evaluable
pool expands, but TN CRR p90=1.07–1.24 is well below 1.55 — few new FPs expected.

What we could do instead:
1. Apply CRR rescue before the sanity check in a v17 CNV caller — no HMM re-run, runs in
   15 min. Directly bypasses the sanity gate for any sample with CRR ≥ 1.55. Risk: MDR1
   and PM2_PM3 failed-pool CRR p90=1.62–1.82, so ~10–15% of the large masked pool (mostly
   GT negatives) would be called amplified, introducing a batch of FPs and hurting PPV.
   Better to fix HMM calibration cleanly if 0.99 works.
2. If 0.99 still fails to drop transitions enough, the next step would be to smooth the
   HMM emission input (rolling average over reconstruction residuals) rather than relying
   solely on the transition prior.

Run time estimate: ~2–3 hours (HMM re-run ~2h + CNV calling ~30 min + evaluation ~5 min;
no training or inference).
